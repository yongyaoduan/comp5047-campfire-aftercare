CAMPFIRE AFTERCARE - REV 3.2

The PDF contains an instruction sheet and 24 part sheets: four plywood profiles (six physical boards) plus all 20 printed part types. Each part sheet has TOP, FRONT and RIGHT orthographic views generated from the actual CAD geometry, with visible edges and dashed hidden edges. All dimensions are mm. The PDF is fitted to the page and is not a 1:1 cutting template.

Every modeled hole/cutout is numbered individually, with its diameter and center coordinates in the schedule. Both perpendicular bores in the M3 corner bracket are included. Plywood mounting holes are not modeled: locate them during assembly to suit the chosen hardware. The purchased tank, funnel and electronics are not fabrication parts in this drawing set.

The adapter bore of 172 mm is provisional and must be revised after measuring the purchased funnel. Fit, load and leak performance have not been physically tested. Use the enclosed current STL files; lower wall panels include the revised 0.4 mm horizontal seam clearance. Actual print compensation and cutting kerf are not included.

The SVG folder contains three vector projections per part. DXF files are 1:1 planar profiles; the bracket DXF is only its horizontal section, not a complete fabrication drawing. Refer to the PDF for its complete three-view definition.
